How To Use This Website

Many have general knowledge about the stock market, but this website is dedicated to providing a deeper understanding of the composition of the stock market and will provide an overview of the top stocks in each sector this year. In addition to illuminating the 11 larger sectors of the market, the user can explore elements of each sector including top performers and may explore details of many of the publicly traded stocks.
