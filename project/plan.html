Site Name

Stock Market Sectors and Data

Site Purpose

The purpose for this website is that I have been creating (on my own time) a personal finance website to teach my children and provide financial resources and tools. This will becomes one more point of information for them as well. The reason that I chose this project is that I’m interested in the financial markets and by building such a site, it combines my interest with the learning of dynamic websites.

Scenarios

  1. What are the sectors of the stock market?
  2. What are the top companies in each sector by market capitalization?
  3. What is the market cap of the top 5 companies of each sector?
  4. What are the fundamentals of a specific company by stock ticker?
